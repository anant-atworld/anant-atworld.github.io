AtWorld

Anant’s World is a personal dashboard for a NEET 2028 student. It combines a daily habit tracker, a spaced revision planner, a study timer, and five life groups (Studies, Chess, Valorant, Books, Music) in one fast, single-file website.

Live site: https://atworld.github.io (replace with your own address if it is different)

Features
Home
Daily habit checklist with 11 habits (wake up without scrolling, gym, breakfast, two study blocks, 100 MCQs, revision, class, capped gaming, reading, sleep by 11 pm)
Progress tracking with a percentage for today, a streak counter (days at 70% or more), and a 7-day chart
NEET countdown showing the days left (approximate date: May 2028)
Revision planner (1-3-5-7): add a topic on the day you study it, and it comes back for revision on day 1, 3, 5, and 7. Chips turn orange when due and green when done.
Study timer with Focus 50, Focus 25, and Break 10 modes. It counts finished focus sessions each day.
Doubt solver link that opens a small Claude-powered page for quick NEET doubts
Five group pages

Each group has its own page. Tap one in the sidebar and only that page opens.

Group	What it tracks
Studies	10th board %, total MCQs solved, study hours this week, last mock score
Chess	Rapid, blitz, and bullet Elo, games this week
Valorant	Rank, hours this week, goal rank
Books	Books read, current book, pages today, plus a books library with a takeaway for each book
Music	Favourite genre, listening minutes, focus playlist
Navigation and search
Sidebar on the right with links to every section and group (a Menu button on phones)
Built-in search that finds sections, habits, stats, revision topics, and books, with a Search the web fallback that opens Google
How to use it
Every day: tick your habits on the Home page. Try to reach 70% or more to keep your streak.
After studying a topic: add it in the revision planner, then come back on the due days and tick each chip.
During study blocks: start a focus timer, keep your phone in another room, and take a break when it ends.
Once a week: update your stats on each group page.
Your data and privacy
Everything you type is saved in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to a server.
Data does not sync between devices. Your phone and laptop each keep their own copy.
Clearing your browser data will erase your progress.
The page is public. Anyone with the link can open it, but they only see the default values, and nothing they type can change your site or your saved data.
Do not put private information (phone numbers, passwords, email) in the page or in this repository.
Built with
Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single index.html file
Fonts: Space Grotesk and Inter from Google Fonts
Hosted free on GitHub Pages
Update the site
Open the repository on GitHub and click index.html.
Click the pencil icon (Edit this file).
Select all, paste the new code, and click Commit changes.
Wait 1 to 3 minutes, then refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R.

To set it up from scratch, create a public repository named yourusername.github.io, upload index.html, then go to Settings → Pages and deploy from the main branch (root).
